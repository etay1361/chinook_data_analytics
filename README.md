
# Chinook Data Analytics & Data Warehouse Project

## Project Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project based on the Chinook digital music store database.

The project combines data preparation, relational data modeling, SQL analysis, and Python-based exploration to identify business patterns and generate actionable insights.

## Tools & Technologies

- **SQL (PostgreSQL)** – Data transformation, joins, CTEs, window functions, and analytical queries.
- **DBeaver** – Database development and SQL execution.
- **Python** – Data analysis and business logic.
- **Pandas** – Data cleaning, aggregation, and manipulation.
- **Matplotlib** – Data visualization.
- **SQLAlchemy** – Database connectivity.
- **Jupyter Notebook / VS Code** – Python development and analysis.

## Data Warehouse

Designed a dimensional data warehouse using staging and DWH schemas.

The model includes customer, employee, track, playlist, and currency dimensions, alongside invoice and invoice-line fact tables.

Primary keys and foreign-key relationships were implemented to support data integrity.

## Independent Analyses & Business Insights

In addition to the required project tasks, I independently designed and implemented four analyses to explore business questions using SQL and Python.

**1. Monthly Sales Patterns (SQL)**

Analyzed monthly sales across the ten highest-revenue countries to identify their strongest and weakest sales months. The analysis highlights differences in monthly performance that may support marketing and promotional planning, while recognizing that these differences alone do not establish recurring seasonality.

**2. Customer Revenue Concentration (SQL)**

Measured the percentage of each country's revenue generated by its top three customers, focusing on countries with at least ten customers. Classified revenue concentration into low, medium, and high categories to assess potential dependence on a small customer base.

**3. Repeat Purchase Behavior (SQL)**

Segmented customers by purchase frequency and compared customer counts, order volumes, and revenue across the groups. The analysis showed that higher-frequency purchase groups generated greater total revenue, highlighting the business importance of repeat customers.

**4. Early-Year Genre Trends (Python)**

Compared the top three music genres sold in January–February 2021 with those sold during March–December across ten selected countries. Found that **80% of the early-year top genres remained in the top three later in the year**, suggesting that early sales patterns may provide useful indicators for marketing decisions. Further analysis across additional years would be needed to establish whether this pattern is consistent.
